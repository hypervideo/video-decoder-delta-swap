How to read this test

Continuous delta swap deliberately sends a delta frame from a separately encoded resolution into the same decoder. It is the stress case—not a production streaming recipe.

PSNR is a tripwire, not a verdict. A sharp drop or a decoder fault marks a suspicious handoff. Use the two video monitors to confirm visible corruption.

Privacy: frames are processed locally with WebCodecs. This page does not upload, record, or persist camera data.
